Get connected in 3 steps

  1. Plug the CANable into the phone with an OTG cable, and its CAN-H / CAN-L into the vehicle's diagnostic or bus wiring.
  2. Switch the ignition on (engine can stay off).
  3. Tap Connect and pick the CANable in the list.

In Monitor mode the app only listens. Pick your vehicle and adapter type in Settings first if they are not the Hunter 350 and a CANable.

    Saved data

    Monitor mode is read-only. It cannot clear or write fault codes. Only the opt-in Diagnostics mode can read and clear standard OBD-II codes; writing, coding and flashing are not implemented at all.

    Diagnostics standard OBD-II only

    Sends only standard OBD-II requests (ISO 15765-4 CAN, 11-bit: 0x7DF to all ECUs, 0x7E0 to 0x7E7 to one ECU, replies 0x7E8 to 0x7EF). There is no writing, security access, routine control, flashing or raw-frame sender.

    Hunter 350 and other motorcycles often use proprietary protocols, so standard OBD may not answer at all (you will see NO DATA). Diagnostics on the Hunter profile is experimental and unverified.

    ELM327 adapter: the dongle builds the ISO-TP frames and flow control itself. ELM327 monitoring is limited; it is mainly for this tab.

    Before you enable Diagnostics

    • Use it only on your own vehicle. You are responsible for what you do with it.
    • Vehicle stationary, parked safely, ignition on, engine off, battery healthy (a weak battery can corrupt ECU operations).
    • This mode transmits on the CAN bus. Only standard OBD-II requests are sent, and the adapter leaves listen-only while it is active.
    • Clearing codes resets the readiness monitors, erases freeze-frame data and can hide a real emissions or safety fault. A car may fail an inspection until its monitors complete again.
    • Hunter 350 and other motorcycles often use proprietary protocols, so standard OBD may not respond. Diagnostics there is experimental and unverified.
