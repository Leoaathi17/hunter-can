Get connected in 3 steps

  1. Plug the CANable into the phone with an OTG cable, and its CAN-H / CAN-L into the vehicle's diagnostic or bus wiring.
  2. Switch the ignition on (engine can stay off).
  3. Tap Connect and pick the CANable in the list.

The app only listens. Pick your vehicle in Settings first if it is not the Hunter 350.

    Connection

    Listen-only always. The only adapter commands ever sent are C (close), S0 to S8 (bitrate), M1 (silent mode), O (open) and V (version). No CAN data frame is ever transmitted.

    Saved data

    Names and decoders live only on this phone, per vehicle profile.

    Read-only. Clearing or writing fault codes needs transmitting, which this app cannot do. Use a proper scan tool for that.
